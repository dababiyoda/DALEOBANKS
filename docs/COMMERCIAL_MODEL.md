# Commercial Model

DALEOBANKS is intended to become a major, possibly the primary, early income
source funding UNIIMENTE development. Nothing in this file is revenue. Every
commercial number in this repository is currently zero, and no fixture may
stand in for one.

## The flywheel

```
audience → trust → distribution → useful products
        → revenue → contribution margin
        → reserves, taxes, obligations
        → founder-authorized free cash flow
        → UNIIMENTE compute, engineering, legal, research
        → better capability → stronger DALEOBANKS → more cash flow
```

`DALEOBANKS_FREE_CASH_FLOW_AVAILABLE_TO_FUND_UNIIMENTE` is a candidate
bottleneck metric for a later stage. It is **not** the current SBM, and it
must never be optimized at the expense of customer value, brand trust, taxes,
reserves, working capital, legal obligations, platform resilience, or
participant welfare. A cash engine that burns the trust producing the cash
has not found a shortcut; it has found an ending.

## Revenue categories

Advertising · sponsorships · memberships · subscriptions · education ·
digital products · events · experiences · merchandise · disclosed affiliate ·
software · services · licensing · research products · media production ·
commerce · brand partnerships · community products · recruiting · talent
discovery · lawful venture referrals · owned incubated businesses ·
ownership stakes · distribution services · lawful privacy-safe data products.

None is mandatory. **Each must earn its complexity.** A category that adds
operational surface without adding margin or capability is a liability
wearing a revenue label.

## What counts as commercial proof

Not proof: followers, impressions, likes, content volume, a shadow receipt, a
passing test, a projection.

Proof: a real buyer, a real offer, a real payment, real delivery, real
acceptance, reconciled economics, and a nonnegative or improving contribution
margin. Repeat purchase and retention outrank first purchase.

No fixture revenue. No simulated payment. A `Conversion` row created by a
test is a test.

## Disclosure

Promoting PumpStation, a UNIIMENTE venture cell, a company Alfonso owns, a
paid sponsor, an affiliate product, or an invested company requires
disclosure appropriate to the surface.

Owned-company promotion may not be dressed as independent editorial analysis.
`IMPLEMENTED`: `services/integrity_guard.assert_disclosed` refuses content
that names a material relationship with no disclosure marker, with a negative
control proving it does not demand disclosure on unrelated content.

Trust compounds. So does its loss, and faster.

## Scorecard families — kept separate on purpose

Collapsing these into one number is how a business talks itself into
believing engagement is revenue.

**Commercial** — revenue, contribution margin, free cash flow, retention,
diversification, free cash flow available to UNIIMENTE.

**Distribution** — qualified reach, owned audience, email conversion,
community conversion, platform concentration, repeat audience.

**Advancement** — verified advancement actions, skills gained, legitimately
measurable income improvements, business-validation actions, ownership
actions, collaborations formed.

**Opportunity** — qualified OpportunityPackets, validated problems, correctly
killed weak ideas, venture-cell candidates. *Correctly killed* is a positive
number.

**Aspiration** — gates cleared, primitives unlocked, external capabilities
absorbed, aspiration-to-reality latency.

**Integrity** — all of these must read zero: known false claims published,
deception events, coercive influence events, undisclosed material
sponsorship, coordinated inauthentic behavior, unauthorized external effects,
founder-intent lineage gaps.

## Order of construction

Owned distribution before monetization. A paid offer sold into rented
distribution converts once and teaches nothing durable.

The gate before any of it is the same six declarations blocking everything
else: surface, timezone, source packet, owned-audience destination, resource
ceiling, stop condition.
