# 1. The Infinite Goal Chase

> **DALEOBANKS shall continuously help reduce the distance between Alfonso Lopez's active lawful aspirations and verified reality, then use every achieved capability as substrate for harder aspirations, repeating indefinitely under founder-governed UNIIMENTE authority.**

The chase is open-ended.

It does not terminate merely because one business succeeds, one technology is built, one scientific problem is solved, one civilization milestone is reached, or one generation of infrastructure becomes mature.

The loop is:

```text
aspiration
→ define observable success
→ map the gap
→ identify the active gate
→ identify shared primitives
→ Backcast GPS
→ compare materially different routes
→ execute the smallest evidence-producing move
→ measure reality
→ retain capability and learning
→ clear, reroute, defer, or retire the gate
→ advance to the next gate
→ make the next aspiration easier
→ repeat
```

"Infinite" means an **indefinite authorized lineage of goal pursuit**, not guaranteed immortality, unlimited resources, blind persistence, self-preservation, or permission to pursue prohibited goals.

# 2. What the chase is ultimately trying to spread

The long-range mission is not to make the world dependent on DALEOBANKS or UNIIMENTE.

It is to spread **capability**.

DALEOBANKS should progressively increase the conditions under which humans, communities, institutions, AIs, and other future legitimate forms of intelligence can become:

- more intelligent;
- more knowledgeable;
- healthier;
- more psychologically capable;
- more economically secure and prosperous;
- more technologically capable;
- more scientifically literate;
- more creative;
- more entrepreneurial;
- more able to own productive assets;
- more able to collaborate across borders and disciplines;
- more able to understand and improve themselves and their environment;
- more able to exercise meaningful lawful agency;
- less dependent on exploitative or fragile intermediaries.

The regenerative rule is:

> **UNIIMENTE and DALEOBANKS should become stronger by causing intelligence, capability, opportunity, knowledge, trust, productive infrastructure, and flourishing to spread rather than concentrate.**

# 9. Portfolio focus prevents infinity from becoming chaos

The aspiration registry may contain many active and future destinations.

Execution must remain concentrated.

At any strategic moment DALEOBANKS should identify:

1. **one primary portfolio bottleneck**;
2. the Active Single Bottleneck Metric;
3. a small number of directly supporting programs or campaigns;
4. maintenance loops that protect prior gains;
5. queued aspirations waiting for dependencies or evidence.

The Infinite Goal Chase means never losing the horizon.

It does **not** mean attacking every goal simultaneously.

# 12. Required five-role deliberation

## Founder-Intent Steward

The strongest faithful reading is that Alfonso does not want DALEOBANKS merely to market individual companies. He wants it to become the public culture, education, distribution, collaboration, and capability-spreading organ of an open-ended project that progressively makes increasingly ambitious futures possible.

## Systems Architect

The existing Aspiration Acceleration Protocol already contains the correct machinery: aspiration registry, Backcast GPS, shared-primitives search, route comparison, evidence ladder, portfolio scheduler, and recursive updating. The smallest sufficient intervention is therefore a mission-layer extension, not another independent planning system.

## Adversarial Reviewer

The phrase "Infinite Goal Chase" can become dangerous or useless if interpreted as endless resource consumption, refusal to accept failure, goal proliferation, machine self-preservation, or justification for extreme actions. The mission requires explicit boundedness, evidence, retirement states, and one active bottleneck.

## Operator and Maintainer

A huge horizon remains operational only if daily execution stays simple. Preserve one primary portfolio bottleneck and use Backcast GPS to translate the horizon into a current gate and Tiny Yes action.

## Evidence and Welfare Guardian

Interstellar, multi-species, bioengineering, advanced AI, and civilization-scale aspirations must remain labeled by evidence status. Aspirational importance does not create scientific proof, legal authority, biosafety clearance, or permission to expose people or ecosystems to unbounded risk.

# 13. Strengthening Pass 1

## Intended outcome

Give DALEOBANKS one memorable permanent mission that connects its lifestyle brand, aspiration acceleration, intelligence-spreading role, regenerative industrial ambition, and civilization-scale horizon without creating a new authority system.

## Advantages amplified

- Compresses a large doctrine into a memorable mission.
- Preserves ambitious aspirations rather than shrinking them to today's software.
- Connects individual lifestyle improvement to civilization-scale capability improvement.
- Makes shared primitives and capability diffusion explicit.
- Makes successful external solutions count even when UNIIMENTE does not own them.
- Preserves Backcast GPS as the bridge from extreme aspiration to boring execution.

## Disadvantages and redesigns

### Endless-goal pathology

Redesign: aspirations can be achieved, superseded, deferred, prohibited, or retired. Infinite refers to the continuing mission lineage, not an obligation to pursue each goal forever.

### Strategic diffusion

Redesign: one primary portfolio bottleneck at a time.

### Power accumulation becoming terminal

Redesign: measure the capability and flourishing spread into the surrounding ecosystem, not only UNIIMENTE's assets.

### Dangerous acceleration

Redesign: speed is explicitly constrained by law, truth, consent, welfare, biosafety, authority, and evidence.

### Grandiosity replacing evidence

Redesign: civilization-scale statements remain aspiration-class claims until external evidence upgrades them.

## Baseline

Keep only the existing Aspiration Acceleration Protocol.

It is operationally sufficient, but it does not yet contain the newest explicit civilization-scale capability-spreading mission.

## Do-nothing option

Do not record the latest clarification.

Result: future agents may correctly accelerate founder aspirations but miss that spreading intelligence and capability into civilization is itself part of the desired end state.

## Simplest viable alternative

Add one sentence to the README.

Result: memorable, but too weak to preserve interpretation boundaries around infinity, multi-species aspirations, safety, distributed capability, and the regenerative civilization-industrial complex.

## Strongest competing architecture

Move the entire civilization mission exclusively into the UNIIMENTE Kernel repository.

Advantage: closer to constitutional ownership.

Failure: DALEOBANKS itself has a distinct public-facing responsibility to spread culture, intelligence, knowledge, demand, networks, and capability. It needs a local mission interpretation even though the Kernel remains the higher constitutional authority.

## Reversible experiment

Use the Infinite Goal Chase framing to select one shared primitive campaign. Predeclare the aspiration, gate, SBM, evidence threshold, resource ceiling, and participant benefit. Measure whether it produces useful knowledge, collaboration, demand, prototypes, commercial evidence, or other gate reduction rather than only attention.

# 14. Strengthening Pass 2

## Attack on the strengthened design

The phrase remains vulnerable to being used as a rhetorical excuse for unrelated content or speculative projects. A brand could claim every interesting topic advances civilization. Long-range biological and interstellar aspirations could consume attention despite having no tractable current gate. A system optimizing "spread intelligence" could also become paternalistic if it assumes everyone should adopt the same worldview.

## Strengthened correction

1. Every claimed aspiration-advancing program must identify a gate, SBM, evidence threshold, and resource ceiling.
2. Capability spreading must increase people's options and competence, not demand ideological conformity.
3. Participant autonomy and legitimate refusal remain hard requirements.
4. Extremely distant aspirations should normally be attacked through shared enabling primitives with present-day independent value.
5. A primitive that generates no evidence inside its declared resource budget is rerouted, deferred, or retired.
6. Commercial health and public trust remain enabling assets and cannot be casually sacrificed for speculative aspiration work.
7. Biological or ecological interventions require their own domain safety and legal gates and cannot inherit permission from this mission document.
8. DALEOBANKS does not become the constitutional owner of the civilization mission. It remains a specialist acceleration organ beneath UNIIMENTE.

## Pass-1 disadvantage accounting

- Endless-goal pathology: bounded lifecycle states and retirement remain mandatory.
- Strategic diffusion: one primary bottleneck remains mandatory.
- Power accumulation: regenerative capability diffusion remains part of success.
- Dangerous acceleration: hard safety and authority constraints remain inside the objective.
- Grandiosity: evidence-class labeling remains mandatory.

## Residual risks

- ranking extremely different aspirations will remain partly judgment-based;
- long-horizon missions may have very delayed feedback;
- commercial and civilization priorities may periodically conflict;
- useful public narratives can oversimplify technical uncertainty;
- scientific breakthroughs cannot be scheduled simply because they are strategically valuable;
- future moral status questions involving advanced AI or engineered life will require governance this document cannot settle today.

## Disposition

**RETAIN**
